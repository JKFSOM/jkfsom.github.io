## How it works

The scoring in this example is plain arithmetic, and you can change the filter or the selected query to see it recalculate. Each query is compared with the median click-through rate at a benchmark position, then the shortfall is converted into estimated missed clicks.

Explanations are the part of this pipeline that would come from a model. Here they were written by hand to show the design, and no model runs in your browser.
