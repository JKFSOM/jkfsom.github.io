[Cavora](https://www.gocavora.com/) checks whether AI shopping agents can find a store's products, read accurate prices and stock, and buy through an advertised agent checkout. Users can request an agentic commerce assessment of their site through the website or over MCP.

## What the assessment covers

The scan groups its checks into finding, understanding and buying:

- **Agent access:** robots rules, Content Signals, HTTP 402 paywalls and simulated agent requests.
- **Findable products:** sitemaps, categories and AI search.
- **Product facts:** price, stock, identifiers, offers and ratings in the page markup.
- **Price and stock match:** whether agents see the same price and stock as shoppers.
- **Basket route:** whether an agent can build a basket from a link and reach the cart.
- **Agent checkout:** whether UCP or ACP checkout is advertised and valid.

Scoring uses fixed rules rather than a model, and every finding links to its evidence. Anything the scan cannot see, such as private feeds or contract pricing, is marked as not verified rather than guessed.
