[Cavora](https://www.gocavora.com/) is an agentic commerce compatibility checker for online stores. It assesses how ready a site is for AI shopping agents, from being found to completing a purchase.

## What it checks

Search engines are no longer the only way shoppers find products. AI assistants now answer questions, compare products and, more and more, buy on the shopper's behalf. Cavora looks at both:

- **SEO:** whether search engines and AI crawlers can reach and index the store.
- **AEO (answer engine optimisation):** whether AI assistants can read accurate product information and recommend the store.
- **Agentic checkout:** whether an agent can get from a product to a completed purchase.

Each finding links to the evidence behind it, and the report ends with prioritised fixes.

## How to request an assessment

Users can request an agentic commerce assessment of their site through [the Cavora website](https://www.gocavora.com/), or over MCP from their own AI assistant.
